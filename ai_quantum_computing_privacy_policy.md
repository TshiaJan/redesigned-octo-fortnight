## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

AI Quantum Computing Platform is designed as an offline-capable, client-side simulator. We operate on a strict data-minimization model:

- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not require or collect your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- **No Mandatory Account Registration:** You can design quantum circuits, configure algorithms, and run simulations immediately without creating an account or logging in.
- **No Precise Location Tracking:** We do not request, access, or log your GPS coordinates or geographical movements.
- **No Biometric Data:** We do not collect fingerprints, facial data, or any biometric markers.
- **No Camera or Microphone Access:** The Application does not record audio, capture images, or access your camera.
- **No Contact Lists or Private Files:** We do not inspect your device contacts, photo library, or local file system.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage on Your Device

To provide a persistent and seamless user experience, simulation parameters and application state are stored locally on your device via standard client-side technologies (such as `localStorage`):

- **Quantum Circuit Designs & Gate Configurations** (Hadamard, CNOT, Pauli-X/Y/Z, Phase gates)
- **Algorithm Parameters & Execution History** (Grover search queries, Shor factorization targets, VQE iterations, QAOA graphs)
- **Simulation Results, Probability Distributions & Fidelity Metrics**
- **User Interface Preferences** (Dark mode theme settings, active tab states)

All configuration and simulation data stays strictly on your device and is never transmitted to external cloud servers. You can clear this data at any time via your browser or device application settings.

## 4. Quantum Simulation and Computational Integrity

All quantum circuit executions, state vector calculations, and probability amplitudes are computed locally or presented as interactive simulations for educational and research exploration. No confidential intellectual property or computational blueprints are uploaded to third parties.
